Design a product concept for a low-cost assistive device for deaf-blind
people. Hard constraints:
- bill-of-materials cost under $50
- usable by someone with no sight and no hearing
- must be operated without any screen, speaker, or keyboard
- must run for 12+ hours on 2 AA batteries

Deliver: (1) a one-paragraph concept, (2) the 5 most important design
decisions with one-line justifications, (3) the single biggest risk and how
you would de-risk it. Be concrete and original; do not restate generic
accessibility talking points.
