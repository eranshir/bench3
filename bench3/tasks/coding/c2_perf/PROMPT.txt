The file `analytics.py` implements two sliding-window functions that are
correct but O(n*w): `rolling_median` and `window_distinct_counts`. On a
150,000-point series with a window of 2,500 they take tens of seconds
(verify with `python3 bench.py`).

Rewrite both functions to be fast — sub-quadratic in n — WITHOUT changing
their observable behaviour: identical outputs on every input, identical
exceptions for invalid input (window <= 0, window > len(values)).

- `rolling_median(values, window)` -> list[float]: median per window, with
  the same even-length averaging as now.
- `window_distinct_counts(values, window)` -> list[int]: distinct count
  per window.

You may import from the standard library and restructure internals, but
keep the module's public names and signatures. Verify correctness and
speed with `python3 bench.py` — after your change both functions should
finish in well under a second on the given sizes.
